Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Toughened glass prices in Banda start from approximately ₹80–₹120 per square foot depending on thickness (5mm, 8mm, 10mm, 12mm) and type (clear, frosted, tinted). The final price depends on the area, design complexity and fittings required. We offer free site visits and detailed quotes — call us for an accurate estimate!
Glass door installation cost depends on the type: frameless glass doors start from ₹8,000–₹15,000 for standard sizes. Sliding doors, pivot doors and custom designs may cost more. All prices include materials, hardware fittings and professional installation. Call us for a free on-site quotation.
Office glass partition cost starts from ₹200–₹350 per square foot for aluminum-framed glass partitions. Frameless or full-height glass partition systems may cost more. Prices include frame, glass, fittings and installation. We serve offices, clinics, banks and commercial spaces across Banda and UP.
Yes! We specialize in commercial glass solutions including shopfronts, office cabins, glass partitions, curtain walls, display windows, conference room glass walls and more. We've completed 100+ commercial projects across Banda, Chitrakoot, Atarra, Karwi and surrounding areas.
We offer complete aluminum fabrication services: sliding & casement windows, aluminum doors, shopfront systems, curtain walls, false ceiling frames, pergolas and custom aluminum structures. All work uses high-quality powder-coated aluminum sections available in white, brown, grey, gold and custom colors.
Most residential glass installations (doors, shower enclosures, railings) are completed within 1–3 days. Commercial projects like full office partitions or glass cabins take 3–7 days depending on scale. We always provide a delivery timeline before starting and stick to it — on-time completion is our promise.
